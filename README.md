## **Project Overview**

Gun violence remains a pressing public safety challenge in the United States. This project examines gun-related incidents across states and cities to uncover underlying trends, emerging patterns, and contributing risk factors.

## **Data**

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1M5sywOjkdZknZYj_ngxHKimwbX_T1gtw?dmr=1&ec=wgc-drive-globalnav-goto

## **Objectives**

* Analyze geographic and temporal patterns in gun violence.
* Assess the relationship between weapon type and fatality rates.
* Apply clustering methods to classify incidents by severity.
* Conduct time-series forecasting to identify seasonal fluctuations.

## **Key Findings**

* Gun violence rates vary widely, with certain states and cities experiencing disproportionately higher incidents.
* Cases involving multiple firearms are strongly associated with higher fatality counts.
* Weekend spikes and seasonal cycles indicate temporal predictability.
* Clustering results highlight distinct categories of incidents, helping law enforcement allocate resources more effectively.

## **Next Steps**

* Integrate socio-economic indicators such as poverty, education, and unemployment for deeper insights.
* Build predictive models to forecast future trends and hotspots.
* Evaluate the effectiveness of state-level gun policies and regulations on incident frequency.
